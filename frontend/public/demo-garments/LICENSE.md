## Purpose in Phase 1
In accordance with `PROJECT_CHARTER.md` and `TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md`, Phase 1 uses these royalty-free, locally bundled assets to verify the UI layout, garment selection, color tokens, and preview panels without depending on external unverified images or live AI endpoints.
